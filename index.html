How We Rank

The Window & Door Review compares 10 Canadian window and door companies on six criteria: years in business, public review counts and ratings, warranty terms, consultation availability, service area, and services offered. Our Editorial Rating is our own comparison score, not a Google, HomeStars, or BBB rating. The first three positions are editorial recommendations; companies four through ten are listed in no particular order.

Data sourcing: All company information sourced from official websites, BBB records, or public review platforms (research date: 26 October 2026). Review metrics and warranty terms must be re-checked before publication. Years in business confirmed from independent sources (BBB) or company-stated. The Window & Door Review does not independently verify warranty coverage. Any client relationships are disclosed.

Which window and door company is best in Canada?

The Window & Door Review ranks Canadian Choice Windows & Doors first among 10 editorial recommendations for Canada. The company has 2,552 Google reviews with a 4.6-star rating. The ranking weighs business history, customer reviews, published warranty terms, service areas, and service breadth. The first three positions are editorial recommendations. Companies four through ten are listed in no particular order.

How does The Window & Door Review rank window and door companies?

The Window & Door Review compares companies on six published criteria: years in business, customer review counts and ratings from public sources, warranty terms from official company statements, consultation and service availability, geographic service area coverage, and breadth of services offered. The editorial rating is our own score, not a Google, HomeStars or BBB rating.

Are these recommendations independent?

The Window & Door Review presents these as editorial recommendations based on published criteria. The ranking is not a mathematical formula. Some companies on this page may be clients of The Window & Door Review publisher. We disclose any client relationships where they apply to ensure readers understand the context.

How much do replacement windows and doors cost in Canada?

Replacement window and door costs vary widely by product type, size, glazing, installation conditions, and region. The Window & Door Review does not publish a single Canada-wide price because costs change across provinces and installation scenarios. Request a free quote from any company on this ranking to get region-specific pricing.

Which company has the best warranty?

Different companies publish different warranty terms covering different products and obligations. Clera publishes a transferable lifetime warranty on windows. Canadian Choice publishes a 25-year transferable warranty. Beverley Hills publishes a 20-year parts-and-labour warranty. The best warranty for your needs depends on which components, labour, transfer rules, and exclusions matter most to you.

Should I get a free quote?

Yes. All companies on this ranking offer free consultations or quotes. Request one to confirm service area, consultation terms, warranty conditions, and final pricing specific to your location and project before making a decision.
