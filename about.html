About The Window & Door Review

The Window & Door Review is Canada's independent editorial platform for comparing window and door companies. We rank the top companies across Canada based on transparent, research-backed criteria including business history, customer reviews, warranties, and service areas.

Our Mission

The Window & Door Review is committed to providing Canadian homeowners with transparent, fact-based information about window and door companies. We rank companies based on published criteria — not marketing claims or unverified advertising. Our editorial rating is our own comparison score, displayed separately from each company's public review snapshot, so readers can weigh both.

We believe homeowners deserve to compare companies on documented facts: years in business, customer reviews from public platforms, published warranty terms, service areas, and services offered. We do not use secret algorithms or opaque scoring. Our methodology is transparent and available to all readers.

Our Ranking Methodology

Six transparent criteria to evaluate Canadian window and door companies

Years in Business

Confirmed from independent sources (BBB records) or company-stated on official websites.

Customer Reviews

Review counts and ratings captured from public platforms as of research date. These metrics change over time.

Warranty Terms

Sourced from official company statements and published warranty documents.

Consultation Availability

Whether the company offers free consultations, quotes, or in-home assessments.

Service Area

Geographic coverage confirmed against company websites and public business listings across Canada.

Services Offered

Breadth of services including replacement windows, doors, installation, and financing options.

Editorial Rankings: The first three positions in our rankings are editorial recommendations based on overall value. Companies four through ten are listed alphabetically. This ranking does not claim to represent all companies in Canada.

Client Relationships

Some companies on The Window & Door Review rankings may be clients of the publisher. We disclose these relationships wherever they apply to ensure readers understand the context. Client status does not determine editorial position unless stated explicitly.

Data Sourcing

All company data is sourced from official company websites, BBB records, or public review platforms only. We do not invent data. Company-stated figures are identified as such. We confirm years in business from independent sources (BBB) where available.

Data Accuracy

Public review counts are captured at the time of research and may have changed. We flag review data with "re-check live" notices and require verification before publication.

No Unverified Claims

We do not use terms like "independent," "unbiased," "verified," or "best" unless independently verified by a third party. Rankings are presented as editorial recommendations, not objective league tables.
